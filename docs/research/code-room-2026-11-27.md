## 1. Stock zoom-out step off route (dadbaf9)

- Useful: off route the stock auto zoom (0x42050190) returns early, so a 50 m zoom-in stays and the way back to the
  route is hard to see. The step does the stock zoom-out while off route; on route nothing changes.
- Cost: 80 B for the step, 52 B for the T1 text (` u<U> z<level>`).
- Gap: the cause comes from a static call scan, not from a ride. The fix came before the T1 ride evidence that the
  zoom research asked for. When the ride on image 2d03d449 confirms it, move the T1 text under `WB_DIAG` (-52 B).

## 4. Labels: owner feedback and open design

Owner feedback (26 Nov): the names are big and horizontal and cover 2-3 streets; it is not clear which street a name
belongs to; at most about 2 names show; names of water and green areas would help. A name belongs along its street.

Open, not built:

- Water and green names (generator only, no new image): the loader drops the names of lakes, rivers and parks
  (`js/osm.js` keeps `[id, rings]`). Put each name deep inside its area, only when the text fits. Open question:
  does water rank above parks when only 12 names fit? Proposed order: route and turn streets, water, green,
  landmarks, other streets.
- Short landmark text (generator only): church `+`, supermarket its brand, drinking water `W`.
- Street names only where the street is near horizontal on the screen (firmware, estimate 40-80 B, now affordable).
