How the unit makes the line
  1. The PC calculates, for each stored street point, the shortest street way to the route. Each point stores only its next point and its distance.
  2. The unit finds the stored street piece nearest to the rider, within 15 m.
  3. The line starts at the nearest point on that piece. Then it goes from point to next point until the route.
Does the rider's direction matter?
  • No. This test image uses the position only. It does not use heading or speed.
  • So the line can start behind the rider. It always goes to the nearest route point by street, not to the route point ahead.
  • The arrows on the grey streets are not one-way signs. They show only the stored direction to the route.
No line (white area)
  • No stored street within 15 m: yard, field, park, building, bad GPS.
  • A street that has no permitted way to the route.
  • On the route itself the stock navigation is active and no way-back line is necessary.
